## 1. Core idea

> Quality is **felt through specifics**, not announced through adjectives.

Instead of *"luxurious bedroom"* → *"king-size bed, hot-tub bath, bathroom floor heating"*.
Instead of *"five-star service"* → *"daily housekeeping, 24-hour reply"*.

The reader infers quality. No label needed.

## 3. Blacklist (DO NOT USE)

### 3.1 Marketing clichés

- ❌ luxury, luxurious
- ❌ 5-star, five-star, world-class, top-tier
- ❌ best, finest, ultimate, premium (as marketing adjective)
- ❌ amazing, incredible, breathtaking, stunning (when describing self)
- ❌ unparalleled, unmatched, exclusive, elite
- ❌ paradise, oasis, sanctuary, haven
- ❌ unforgettable, magical, dream

### 3.2 Hype patterns

- ❌ "the most ___ in [city/category]"
- ❌ "you'll never forget"
- ❌ "your dream [vacation / experience]"
- ❌ "imagine yourself…"
- ❌ ALL CAPS for emphasis
- ❌ Emoji
- ❌ Multiple exclamation marks

### 3.3 Vague claims

- ❌ "world-renowned" (unless objectively true with proof)
- ❌ "centuries of tradition"
- ❌ "everything you need" (lazy)

## 4. Whitelist (PREFERRED)

### 4.1 Safe adjectives

- ✅ private, ensuite, spacious, considered, curated
- ✅ heated, full-service, on-call, hand-picked, sourced
- ✅ refined, quiet, calm, easy, comfortable
- ✅ family-run, owner-operated (if true)

### 4.2 Specifics over labels

Examples of specific language to reach for:

- **Numbers**: *5-floor*, *6 ensuite bedrooms*, *400 m²*, *12 guests*
- **Brand names**: *[only when accurate]*
- **Materials**: *travertine*, *oak*, *linen*, *Carrara marble*
- **Distances**: *8-minute walk to Old Town*, *25 min to airport*
- **Time-bound promises**: *reply within 24 hours*, *check-in from 3pm*

| "The ultimate [city] getaway" | "[City], eight minutes from the walls" |

## 6. Sentence rhythm

Mix lengths: a short sentence (≤ 6 words), a medium (10–15), a long (≤ 25). Variety reads better than monotone.

**Good rhythm**:
> Six bedrooms. Five floors. Two heated pools, one for every mood.
> The Old Town walls are eight minutes on foot — closer when the wind carries the cathedral bells.

**Flat rhythm** (avoid):
> Villa T is a luxury villa in Dubrovnik that has six bedrooms and five floors and two heated pools and is close to the Old Town.

## 7. CTA rules

- Start with a verb.
- ≤ 3 words ideal.
- Match the action to the outcome.

| ✅ | ❌ |
|---|---|
| `Check Availability` | `Book Your Dream Stay` |
| `Send Inquiry` | `Submit` |
| `See the Room` | `Click here` |
| `Plan Your Stay` | `Start your unforgettable journey` |

Full CTA dictionary in `_foundations/ux-patterns.md`.

## 8. Numbers and specifics

- Numbers ≤ 12 → write out (`six bedrooms`, `eight minutes`).
- Numbers > 12 or with units → digits (`€450 / night`, `25 minutes`, `400 m²`).
- Currency before number (`€450`).
- Time format: `8 min · 24 hours · 3pm`.
